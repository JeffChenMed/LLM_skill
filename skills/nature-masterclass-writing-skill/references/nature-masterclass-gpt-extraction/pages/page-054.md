## Writing Principles

- Conclusions should relate findings back to the study’s objectives and clarify their significance.
- Avoid redundancy in the Discussion and Conclusions; rather than restating results, interpret them in a broader context.
- Employ the ‘topdown’ approach, presenting the main message at the beginning of each paragraph for clarity.
- Reference lists should be selective and representative, supporting the manuscript’s claims without excess.
- Ensure references conform to the journal’s formatting requirements and maintain a balanced citation approach (not overly self-referential).
- Selecting an appropriate journal requires alignment with the manuscript’s audience, scope, and quality.
- Adherence to the journal’s submission guidelines is crucial; this includes following instructions and preparing all required documentation, such as the cover letter and reviewer suggestions.
