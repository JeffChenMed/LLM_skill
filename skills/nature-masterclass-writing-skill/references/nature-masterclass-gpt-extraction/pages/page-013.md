## Writing Principles

- Clearly define all graphical elements (symbols, patterns, lines, colors, abbreviations, scale bars, error bars) within the figures or legends to ensure clarity and prevent misinterpretation.
- Always specify statistical indicators such as P-values and sample size when applicable.
- Include statistics wherever possible to enable evaluation of the robustness and validity of the data.
- Reference authoritative sources for statistical reporting standards if unsure.
- Ensure any processing of images presented in figures is minimal; any modifications must:
  - Affect the entire image, not just parts.
  - Be explicitly stated in the figure legend.
  - Be documented with full experimental details (equipment, software, settings).
  - Be supplemented with the original, unprocessed images.
- Follow journal-specific guidelines for figure and image preparation and reporting.

## Style / Structure Notes

- Use clear, itemized lists when specifying requirements or recommendations.
- Provide parenthetical clarification for potentially ambiguous terms (e.g., standard deviation or standard error for error bars).
- Refer readers to reputable external sources for supplementary guidance.
- Introduce new sections with transitional phrases (“Following on from this…”, “Finally…”).
- Convey requirements in explicit, actionable bullet points for clarity.
