## Writing Principles

- **Elements of Writing Style:** Focus on the golden rules underlying clear scientific writing and text structure within a paper. Seek clarity, conciseness, and logical organization.
- **Titles and Abstracts:** Titles and abstracts should be both engaging and brief. Aim for precision and interest to capture reader attention quickly.
- **Peer Review:** Understand the editorial and peer review process, including how to interpret and respond to reviewer comments constructively, and how to write effective rebuttal letters.
- **Plagiarism and Ethics:** Be aware of what constitutes unethical writing (e.g., plagiarism), why it occurs, how to recognize it, and steps to address or avoid it.
