## Writing Principles

- Use a “top-down” paragraph structure where the first sentence provides a clear summary of the paragraph’s topic and content.
- The introduction should specify the subject or argument to be discussed.
- Expand on the topic in the body of the paragraph by giving evidence or explaining how the initial statement was derived.
- Often, the final sentence should underline an important outcome or consequence related to your findings.
- Every sentence should be able to stand alone and make sense without relying on surrounding context.
- Avoid overly complex sentences that lose clarity when isolated.
- Use specific examples and connect them directly to your main claim.
- Contrasting terms (like “in contrast”) can be used for emphasis and to highlight distinctive features of your data or argument.
