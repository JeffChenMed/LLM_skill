## Writing Principles

- When listing references, include full citations: author(s), title, journal or source, volume, page numbers, year, and digital object identifier (DOI) or URL if applicable.
- Use consistent formatting (e.g., italics for journal names, consistent order and punctuation).
- Cite blog posts and non-academic web resources with a clear label and direct URL.
- For scientific articles, provide complete bibliographic detail: all authors as “et al.” for multiple authors, full title of the article, full name of the journal (often abbreviated according to standard style), year, volume, and pages.
- When referencing animal models or chemical substances in titles, retain precise scientific nomenclature (e.g., gene names, animal strains).
