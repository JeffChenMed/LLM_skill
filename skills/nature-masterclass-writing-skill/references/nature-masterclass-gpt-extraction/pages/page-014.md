## Writing Principles

- Always assess and ensure high overall figure quality before submission or presentation.
- Avoid using red and green color combinations in figures, as they can be problematic for colorblind readers.
- When reporting P values:
  - Use a capitalized, italic "P".
  - Do not use initial zeros (e.g., P = .05 instead of P = 0.05).
- When reporting R values:
  - Use a capital, non-italic "R" with initial zeros allowed.
  - For "r" values, use lower case, non-italic "r" with initial zeros allowed.
- Clearly label all axes on figures, and make sure the label orientation is appropriate for easy reading.
- Ensure that all panel figures in a composite figure are properly aligned for a neat and professional presentation.
