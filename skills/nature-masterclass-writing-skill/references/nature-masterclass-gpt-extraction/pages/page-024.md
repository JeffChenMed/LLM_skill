## Writing Principles

- Avoid overloading your writing with excessive detail, especially numbers and acronyms.
- Only use abbreviations or acronyms repeatedly if they are used often enough to justify them; otherwise, write terms out in full.
- Expand or clarify all acronyms at first use, but consider whether they are needed at all if used infrequently.
- Round numbers where possible to aid readability (e.g., approximate rather than very precise numbers if the precision is not important).
- Focus on including information that is pivotal to understanding your study; omit details that do not significantly add to the main message.
- Ensure that crucial methodological details (such as sample size) are not omitted.
